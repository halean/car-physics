# Historical seed: Benz Patent-Motorwagen, 1886

Mercedes-Benz documents a two-seat vehicle completed in 1885 with a tubular steel
frame, three wire-spoked wheels and a rear-mounted horizontal single-cylinder
four-stroke engine. Its account places the first public outing in July 1886. [S5]

These features motivate experiment 001. We use a single front steering wheel,
a bench, a slatted floor and an exposed horizontal flywheel as recognizable
visual cues. Exact dimensions, spoke lacing, control routing, finishes and engine
details are modeling approximations. No historical drag coefficient is assigned.

The parameter file uses a 1.45 m wheelbase, 1.19 m rear track, 0.50 m rear rolling
radius and 0.36 m front rolling radius as **assumptions**. They are deliberately
editable and are not presented as measured specifications.

Consult the official page's photographs before refining silhouettes. Do not mix
later Motorwagen variants into a purported precise reconstruction. The present
model is a stylized study with simplified transmission, no working suspension,
and no functional engine internals. [Source index](sources.md)
