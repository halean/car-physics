# Source register

Consulted 2026-09-28. Notes are original summaries; images are not redistributed.
NASA's aircraft teaching material supplies general fluid mechanics, not measured
road-car performance. Automotive modeling implications are our own applications.

- **S1** NASA Glenn, [Drag Equation](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/drag-equation/): dynamic pressure, drag coefficient and reference area.
- **S2** NASA Glenn, [Drag](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/drag/): pressure and skin friction contributions.
- **S3** NASA Glenn, [Boundary Layer](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/boundary-layer/): no slip, Reynolds number, separation.
- **S4** NASA Glenn, [Shape Effects on Drag](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/shape-effects-on-drag/): geometry and controlled coefficient comparisons.
- **S5** Mercedes-Benz Group, [Benz Patent Motor Car, 1885–1886](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/company/tradition/company-history/1885-1886.html): historical layout and photographic reference.
- **S6** Blender, [Command Line Arguments](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/3.0/advanced/command_line/arguments.html): background execution and Python error exit code. Installed CLI help is authoritative for the installed version.
- **S7** Wikipedia, [Benz Patent-Motorwagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benz_Patent-Motorwagen) (consulted 2026-09-28; secondary, citing Oswald and Benz's *Lebensfahrt*): Nr. 1 drive path (bevel gears, uncrossed belt to a split-pulley countershaft with differential, roller chains), belt-shift clutch, 954 cc, 0.68 PS (0.50 kW) at 400 rpm, 270 kg curb weight. The Mercedes-Benz pages refused automated access, so these figures are not yet checked against S5.

- **S8** The Motor Museum in Miniature, [1894 Benz Velo](https://www.themotormuseuminminiature.co.uk/1894-benz-velo.php) (consulted 2026-09-28; secondary): 1340 mm wheelbase, 1000/1040 mm track, 2.25 m length, 550/850 mm wire wheels, 1045 cc 1.5 hp at 450 rpm rear engine, belt to countershaft with chains, two forward speeds, double-pivot steering (DRP 73515) from a central column.
- **S9** Wikipedia, [Benz Velo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benz_Velo) (consulted 2026-09-28): 280–320 kg, 19 km/h. Its 4.4–5.2 N m torque figure is inconsistent with 1.1 kW at 450 rpm and is not used.
- **S10** Jalopnik, [The oldest street-legal car in Germany has two brakes](https://www.jalopnik.com/the-oldest-street-legal-car-in-germany-has-two-brakes-a-1840112407/) (consulted 2026-09-28): an 1894 Benz Victoria with a hand and a foot brake. Brake locations for the Velo remain unsourced.

- **S11** The Motor Museum in Miniature, [The 1891 Panhard et Levassor](https://www.themotormuseuminminiature.co.uk/1891-panhard.php) (consulted 2026-09-29; secondary): Daimler-licence vertical V-twin in front of a friction clutch and sliding three-speed gearbox, chain drive to the rear wheels, hand-lever leather-faced wooden block brakes on the rear wheel rims and a sprag, wooden chassis, unequal wheels.
- **S12** Wikipedia, [Panhard et Levassor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panhard_et_Levassor) (consulted 2026-09-29): Système Panhard layout (front engine, rear-wheel drive, sliding-gear transmission); the steering wheel was fitted to a Panhard in 1894.
- **S13** Carfolio, [1891 Panhard et Levassor](https://www.carfolio.com/panhard-et-levassor--123046): 1.1 L two-cylinder, 4 bhp, 19 km/h. Seen only as a search-result snippet; the page refused automated access, so these figures are unverified.

- **S14** Association Renault Histoire, [La voiturette Type A](https://sites.google.com/view/associationrenaulthistoire/un-peu-d-histoire/les-modeles-phares-de-renault-par-michel-jullien/breve-histoire-de-la-voiturette) (consulted 2026-09-29): front De Dion-Bouton single, 270 cc, 1.75 hp; three speeds plus reverse with direct drive in third; cardan shaft, rear axle and differential; handlebar steering; one pedal for brake and declutch; 1900 × 1150 × 1400 mm; 250 kg; 50 km/h.
- **S15** Wikipedia, [Renault Voiturette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renault_Voiturette) (consulted 2026-09-29): 273 cc 1.75 CV De Dion-Bouton engine, three gears, 200 kg, 32 km/h, Continental tyres.
- **S16** Wikipedia, [Mercedes 35 hp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercedes_35_hp) (consulted 2026-09-29; secondary, citing Eckermann): 5.9 L four, 35 PS at 950 rpm (300–1000 rpm), four speeds plus reverse with gate change, chain drive, hand brake on rear drums and a water-cooled foot brake on the countershaft, pressed-steel U-section ladder frame, rigid axles on semi-elliptic springs, honeycomb radiator, 2,345 mm wheelbase, 1200 kg, 70–75 km/h. Its 2,766 mm length conflicts with the wheelbase and wheel sizes and is not used. Track, ratios and spring rates remain unsourced.
- **S17** Wikipedia, [Ford Model T](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Model_T) (consulted 2026-09-29): 2.9 L four, 20 hp; two-speed planetary transmission with three floor pedals (low, reverse, transmission brake) and a lever (high-speed clutch and parking brake); transmission brake only, hub band brakes on the lever; torque tube with a single universal joint; a transverse semi-elliptic spring on each beam axle; 100 in wheelbase, 56 in track, 30 in wheels; 540–750 kg; 68 km/h. The planetary and bevel ratios and the ball positions remain unsourced.
- **S18** Wikipedia, [Lancia Lambda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancia_Lambda) (consulted 2026-09-29; secondary): produced 1922–1931 in nine series; first series V4 at 13°, 2121 cc, 49 PS at 3250 rpm; the first load-bearing unitary body; independent front suspension by sliding pillars with coil springs; four-wheel brakes; three speeds (S.1–S.4); wheelbase 3.10 m; 1200–1350 kg.
- **S19** Classic & Sports Car, [Lancia Lambda at 100](https://www.classicandsportscar.com/features/lancia-lambda-100-celebrating-italys-pioneer) (consulted 2026-09-29): fourth-series figures, 1065 kg, 3100 mm wheelbase, 1661 mm width, 49 bhp, 68 mph; front independent by coil springs and sliding pillars; rear live axle on semi-elliptic springs with friction dampers; drum brakes. Its 1065 kg conflicts with S18's 1200–1350 kg.
- **S20** autoevolution, [Remembering the pioneering Lancia Lambda](https://www.autoevolution.com/news/remembering-the-pioneering-lancia-lambda-1922-1931-179171.html) (consulted 2026-09-29): the sliding-pillar unit combines the spring and a hydraulic damper; four-wheel mechanical drum brakes; the hull is a welded and riveted steel shell with a central tunnel for the drive shaft and a transverse tunnel for the rear axle.
- **S21** Wikipedia, [Citroën Traction Avant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citro%C3%ABn_Traction_Avant) (consulted 2026-09-29; secondary, many uncited specifications): 7A of 1934 with a 1303 cc engine (72 × 80 mm); three-speed gearbox with a dashboard lever; welded unitary body; torsion-bar and wishbone front suspension; rear beam axle with a Panhard rod, trailing arms and torsion bars on a 75 mm steel tube.
- **S22** Stellantis media, [Citroën celebrates 90 years of the Traction Avant](https://www.media.stellantis.com/em-en/citroen/press/citroen-celebrates-90-years-of-the-traction-avant-an-iconic-model-with-100-patents) and motor1, [Citroën Traction Avant (1934–1957)](https://www.motor1.com/news/717732/citroen-traction-avant-1934-history/) (consulted 2026-09-29, via search summaries: the pages refused direct fetching): 32 PS, 1303 cc, three speeds, about 95 km/h; front-wheel drive with monocoque construction, independent suspension and hydraulic brakes; the transmission mounted ahead of the engine and the front axle; worm-and-roller steering replaced by rack and pinion in May 1936; 2910 mm wheelbase for the 7 and 11 Légère.
- **S23** I. Stokes, [Citroën Traction Avant](https://www.uvm.edu/~istokes/traction/) (owner's technical page, consulted 2026-09-29): all-steel welded monocoque body with a flat floor and no transmission tunnel; hydraulic brakes; torsion-bar suspension; front drive shafts with six universal joints in all, the outer joints double-Cardan type with a centring ball, at the hubs.
- **S24** Wikipedia, [Torsion bar suspension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torsion_bar_suspension) (consulted 2026-09-29): the Traction Avant as the first series-production car with torsion-bar suspension, front independent and rear a trailing dead axle; front torsion bars mounted lengthwise with the lower wishbones as their levers.
- **S25** Wikimedia Commons photographs of 1934 Citroën 7A cars, consulted 2026-09-29 as styling references for the bodywork only (not copied or redistributed): [1934 Citroen Traction Avant 7A](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1934_Citroen_Traction_Avant_7A.jpg) (kitmasterbloke, CC BY 2.0); [1934 Citroën 7A Traction Avant](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1934_Citro%C3%ABn_7A_Traction_Avant_(54180799695).jpg) (Flickr, CC BY 2.0); [CITROEN Type 7A 1934](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:CITROEN_Type_7A_1934.jpg) and [its flank](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:CITROEN_Type_7A_1934_flank.jpg) (Abxbay, CC BY-SA 4.0); [Citroen 7 Legere c.1934](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Citroen_7_Legere_Traction_Avant_c.1934_(16406655927).jpg) (Flickr, CC BY 2.0); [Citroen Type 7 A 1934](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Citroen_Type_7_A_1934_IMG_3295_fransk.jpg) (CC BY-SA 3.0); [Citroën Type 7A 1934 – Autoworld](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Citro%C3%ABn_Type_7A_1934_-_Autoworld.jpg) (CC BY-SA 4.0). Read from them: a short, steep, rounded back; a long flat roof with a small visor; a high belt line; a bonnet tapering to a tall, narrow, raked grille with a rounded top; full front wings with deep outer skirts; rear wings flared outboard of the body; a narrow running board; pressed-steel disc wheels; two-tone paint on several cars.
